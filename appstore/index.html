App Privacy

Data Not Collected. In App Store Connect → App Privacy, answer “No, we do not collect data from this app”.

Progress is saved only on the device. Location (prayer times) and photos (avatar colours) are used on the device and never sent anywhere. No analytics, ads, accounts or chat.

Age rating

Answer None to every content question and No to web access, contests, gambling and user-generated content. The result is 4+.

For the Kids Category Apple also requires no links out of the app and no third-party ads or analytics without a parental gate. Inside the app there are no links out; the parent area is behind a grown-up check.

Notes for the reviewer

Submission checklist

  1. Join the Apple Developer Program and sign in to App Store Connect.
  2. Apps → + → New App: platform iOS, name, primary language, bundle ID com.babymo.islandcraft, SKU.
  3. In Xcode, Product → Archive, then Distribute App → App Store Connect → Upload. Wait for the build to finish processing.
  4. App Information: categories, Kids Category if wanted, privacy policy URL, age rating questionnaire.
  5. App Privacy: Data Not Collected.
  6. Pricing and Availability: Free, choose countries.
  7. Version page (English): screenshots on the “iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)” and “iPad 13″ display” tabs, promotional text, description, keywords, support URL, What's New, copyright.
  8. Add the Indonesian localization and paste its text and screenshots.
  9. Select the uploaded build, add the reviewer notes, answer “No” to export compliance (already set in the app).
  10. Add for Review → Submit. Review usually takes one to three days.
