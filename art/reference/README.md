# Character art references

Renders of the blocky Baby Mo family, kept as references for future art. They are not shipped with the game.

| File | What it is | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `baby-mo-run.png` | Baby Mo in his green outfit, waving and walking (cut-out) | The opening page (`public/img/baby-mo-craft.webp`) and the app icon (`tools/icon/make-icon.mjs`) |
| `girl-pink-hijab-run.png` | The girl in the pink hijab and star outfit, walking (cut-out) | Kept for later |
| `dad-run.png` | The dad with a beard, white shirt and backpack, walking (cut-out) | Kept for later |
| `boy-blue-hoodie-turnaround.png` | The boy in the blue hoodie: front, side and back | Kept for later (a model sheet for a 3D character) |
| `family-run.png` | Dad, Baby Mo and the girl walking together | Kept for later (a banner or store picture) |

The cut-outs had small coloured specks left from the background removal; these copies are cleaned (only the character is kept).
